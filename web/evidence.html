Recorded QNX 8.0 simulation · QEMU/KVM · 4 vCPUs

Controller recovery

One documented 2 mL dose at 360 mL/h finished with 2,000,000 nL delivered in the simulation after three restarts. The motor encoder provided the delivered-volume counter after each restart.

Controller exit

18 ms to restart after the controller was killed.

Controller hang

63 ms to detect the missed heartbeat and recover.

CPU starvation

12 ms to restart. The simulated motor watchdog stopped the pump once.

Budgeted load

The 15-second window had about 1 ms of worst lateness per second, with one 7 ms late tick when the load process spawned.

Idle QEMU

The notes record a 5 ms late tick every few seconds even at idle. Timing depends on the host scheduler and guest setup.
