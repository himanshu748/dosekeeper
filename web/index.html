How the simulated controller recovers

01

The dose lives in hardware, not RAM

Delivered volume is read back from the motor encoder and the prescription is checkpointed in CRC-protected shared memory. A restarted controller resumes the same infusion without losing or repeating a single nanolitre, and the motor refuses to move past the prescribed volume.

02

A watchdog that notices in milliseconds

The guardian process spawns the controller, respawns it the instant it dies, and kills it if its 100 Hz heartbeat pulses stop for 40 ms. The motor has its own 100 ms hardware watchdog as the last line of defence.

03

CPU budgets the scheduler enforces

Background work runs under QNX sporadic scheduling: 2 ms of high priority per 10 ms, then it drops below the controller. Turn that off and the rogue load starves the controller — you can watch both on the live console.
